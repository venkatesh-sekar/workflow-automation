## Iteration #1 - theme-colors
- **Change:** Updated all :root CSS custom properties to match design system — primary shifted from pure red (0° 72% 51%) to muted warm red (4° 55% 48%), neutrals shifted from true gray to cool blue-gray (hue 220°), background to off-white #FAFAFA, ring color matches primary, accent differentiated from muted (93% vs 96% lightness), sidebar and builder tokens aligned
- **Heuristic:** Consistency and Standards (#4), Aesthetic and Minimalist Design (#8)
- **Files changed:** packages/web/src/styles.css
- **Learnings:** Secondary token inversion (dark→light) will break button.tsx spinner visibility and data-table-select-popover checkbox. These need fixing in upcoming iterations. Always differentiate --accent from --muted by 3+ lightness points for hover visibility.

## Iteration #7 - dashboard-layout
- **Change:** Removed custom verbose shadow from main content card — the card already has border + background contrast (bg-background on bg-sidebar), making the shadow redundant. Design spec says "prefer single-pixel borders over shadows for separation" and "Cards: shadow-sm or no shadow (use border instead)."
- **Heuristic:** Aesthetic and Minimalist Design (#8)
- **Files changed:** packages/web/src/app/components/project-layout/index.tsx
- **Learnings:** Content cards on bg-sidebar have 3 separation signals (color contrast, border, shadow). Shadow is redundant — remove it entirely, don't downgrade to shadow-sm. The spec prohibition is absolute for card-level separation.
